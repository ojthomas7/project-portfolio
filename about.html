About Me

Hi, I’m Owen Thomas, a physicist and programmer with a passion for quantum computing, machine learning, and innovative problem-solving. I recently completed my BSc in Physics, where I explored numerical simulations and experimental techniques like Background Oriented Schlieren analysis. My projects blend theoretical physics with practical applications, from building quantum algorithms to classifying electron orbitals with TensorFlow.

Beyond academics, I’ve worked on independent projects like web scraping tools and hardware automation during a research internship. I’m driven by curiosity and enjoy tackling complex challenges with elegant solutions. When I’m not coding or researching, you can find me stargazing or diving into the latest advancements in AI and quantum tech. Feel free to explore more about me and my work here.
